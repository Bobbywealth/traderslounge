# Scoring Methodology

## Overview

Traders Lounge uses a multi-factor confluence scoring system to evaluate trading opportunities across forex, metals, and cryptocurrency markets.

## Score Components

The total confluence score is composed of up to 9 evidence categories:

| Category | Max Points | Description |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| Structure | 20 | Higher timeframe trend and market structure |
| Momentum | 10 | RSI and price momentum indicators |
| Volume | 10 | Volume analysis and profile |
| Moving Averages | 10 | EMA stack alignment |
| Fibonacci | 10 | Key retracement and extension zones |
| Patterns | 10 | Candlestick and chart patterns |
| Volatility | 10 | ATR and volatility regime |
| Relative Strength | 5 | Cross-asset correlation |
| Liquidity | 15 | Order flow and liquidity zones |

## Confidence Framework

Every analysis includes a confidence tier based on data coverage:

| Coverage | Max Tier | Description |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| < 50% | Watch | Limited evidence available |
| 50-74% | Developing | Moderate evidence available |
| 75-89% | Qualified | Strong evidence available |
| >= 90% | High | Comprehensive evidence available |

## Missing Data Behavior

Missing data is NOT treated as negative evidence. Categories without sufficient data simply contribute 0 points without penalizing the score.

## Score Calibration

Scores are calibrated through walk-forward analysis. Higher scores should consistently outperform lower scores in forward-tested results.
